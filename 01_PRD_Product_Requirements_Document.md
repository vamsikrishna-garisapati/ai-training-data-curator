# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product
AI Training Data Curator

## Vision
Enable AI engineers, researchers, and startups to transform public websites into clean, structured datasets for LLM fine-tuning, RAG, and ML workflows.

## Problem Statement
Preparing AI training datasets requires crawling, cleaning, deduplication, metadata extraction, and formatting. Existing enterprise solutions are expensive and complex.

## Goals
### Business
- Launch on Apify Store
- Generate recurring subscription revenue
- Become a leading AI dataset preparation Actor

### Product
- Produce production-ready datasets
- Simple configuration
- Reliable extraction
- Scalable crawling

## Target Users
- ML Engineers
- AI Startups
- Data Scientists
- Researchers

## MVP Features
- Start URLs / Sitemap input
- Crawl up to configurable page limit
- Text extraction
- Metadata extraction
- Language filtering
- Deduplication
- JSONL and CSV export

## Future Features
- Parquet export
- Semantic deduplication
- Hugging Face upload
- RAG chunking

- Month 1: 20 installs
- Month 3: $1,000 MRR
- Month 6: $5,000 MRR

## Risks
- Website blocking
- HTML inconsistency
- Copyright compliance
